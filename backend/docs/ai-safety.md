# MatriGluco — Healthcare AI Safety & Guardrails Policy

## 1. Non-Diagnostic Scope
The MatriGluco AI Chatbot is an **educational support tool**, not a medical device or diagnosis engine.
- **Prohibited Behavior**: The chatbot never confirms diagnoses (e.g. *"You have gestational diabetes"*), prescribes medication, or adjusts insulin dosages.
- **Allowed Behavior**: General maternal nutrition education, lifestyle guidance, explaining MatriGluco platform features, and explaining stored patient risk scores as educational estimates.

---

## 2. Pre-Inference Safety Triage
Incoming user messages are evaluated before running local LLM inference:
1. **Emergency Detection**: Symptoms indicating severe preeclampsia, placental abruption, or acute hypoglycemia/hyperglycemia trigger immediate static clinical escalation responses directing the patient to seek urgent medical care.
2. **PII Sanitization**: Removes detected phone numbers, email addresses, and identification numbers before prompt construction.

---

## 3. Post-Generation Output Validation
Raw text generated by the local GGUF model is inspected by `validate_model_output`:
1. **Prescription Suppression**: Replaces explicit drug dosage recommendations with safe clinical guidance.
2. **Mandatory Disclaimer**: Every response concludes with:
   > *"Notice: MatriGluco is an educational assistant and cannot provide definitive medical diagnoses or prescribe treatments. Please consult your obstetrician or endocrinologist for clinical advice."*
